## 1. Placement and validity

Show the catalogue, held preview and operation controls. Ideally include an invalid placement and the feedback from confirming it.

English caption: “Building placement combines the selected object, its world preview and context-dependent controls. Invalid placement produces feedback without creating the object.”

## 2. Input-mode transition

Show opening/closing the catalogue or switching between panel interaction and world placement. If video is unavailable, use separately labelled stills rather than claiming they demonstrate the transition.

English caption: “The available input follows the current UI and building mode, keeping world actions distinct from menu navigation.”

## 3. Processing station

Show recipe selection, quantity/material information, a queue and a collectable result. A missing-material state is especially useful.

English caption: “The processing panel connects recipe requirements to production actions. Missing materials lead to tracking; queued work and collectable output follow the building's live data.”

## 4. Storage box

Show the box and player containers together, with a transfer or item-detail action when possible.

English caption: “Storage offers quick transfer, item-detail actions and drag/drop over the same native container commands. The interface refreshes from container state.”

Keep original creator watermarks on public-footage captures, record the video URL and playback timestamp, and identify unknown versions. English captions should explain Chinese controls outside the image. Do not substitute generated UI for footage of the real feature. Images from different sessions are not a continuous flow recording.
